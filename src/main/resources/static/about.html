About EEC BookHub

EEC BookHub is a smart student-to-student book bank built for Easwari Engineering College — a place where a book you've finished with can immediately help a classmate, at zero cost, with the platform handling requests, due dates and the occasional late-return penalty automatically.

Why we built this

Every semester, usable textbooks get bought new and later forgotten in hostel rooms. BookHub keeps them circulating instead.

Who can join

Any student with a verified Easwari Engineering College account — no one outside the college can register.

How penalties work

Borrowing is always free. Only late returns generate a small per-day penalty, paid securely through the app when a late-return penalty exists.
