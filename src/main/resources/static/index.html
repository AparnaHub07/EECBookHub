A book bank run by students, for students

Every semester, good textbooks and novels sit unused after exams while other students go buy fresh copies. EEC BookHub turns that around — any Easwari student can lend a book they own, and any Easwari student can borrow it for free, directly from a classmate.

📉 Lower expenses

Borrow textbooks and reference material for free instead of buying new copies every semester.

🌱 Sustainability

Reusing books already on campus means fewer books printed, bought, and discarded.

🤝 Student collaboration

Connect with classmates from other departments and years through shared reading.

🔒 Verified & exclusive

Only verified student accounts can join, so it stays within the EEC community.

How it works

From listing a book to closing the loan, every step happens inside BookHub.

➕

1. Provide a book

List a book you own with its condition, category and preferred duration.

🔍

2. Find & request

Search by title, author or category, then send a borrow request in one tap.

✅

3. Owner approves

The provider accepts or rejects — they always stay in control of their own book.

📅

4. Return on time

A due date is set automatically — return on time for zero penalty, or renew ahead of it.
